Problem

The triangle \( ABC \) is shown below.

Which of the following statement is correct?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Find the missing angles

We are given sides \(b=25\), \(a=15\), and angle \(\angle B = 53^\circ\). Use the Sine Rule to find \(\angle A\):

\[ \frac{\sin A}{15} = \frac{\sin 53^\circ}{25} \]
\[ \sin A = \frac{15 \times \sin 53^\circ}{25} \approx 0.4791 \]
\[ \angle A \approx 28.6^\circ \]
Now find \(\angle C\): \(180^\circ - (53^\circ + 28.6^\circ) = 98.4^\circ\).
Since no angle is \(90^\circ\), this proves Option B is false.

Step 2: Find the missing side

Use the Sine Rule again to find the length of the unknown third side, \(c\) (side \(AB\)):

\[ \frac{c}{\sin 98.4^\circ} = \frac{25}{\sin 53^\circ} \]
\[ c = \frac{25 \times \sin 98.4^\circ}{\sin 53^\circ} \approx 31.0 \text{ cm} \]
Because 31.0 is greater than 25, \(AC\) is NOT the longest side. This proves Option A is false.

Step 3: Check Perimeter and Area

Now evaluate the remaining statements using our calculated values.

Perimeter: \[ 15 + 25 + 31.0 = 71 \text{ cm} \]
Area: \[ \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C = 0.5 \times 15 \times 25 \times \sin 98.4^\circ \approx 185.5 \text{ cm}^2 \] (This proves Option D is false)

Therefore, Option C is correct.
