Problem

What is the value of the following?

\[ \frac{2}{\log_a x} - \frac{1}{\log_b x} \]

Worked Solution

Step 1: Change of Base

Look at your reference sheet for the Change of Base formula: \( \log_a x = \frac{\log_b x}{\log_b a} \). If we use \( x \) as the new base, it becomes \( \log_a x = \frac{\log_x x}{\log_x a} = \frac{1}{\log_x a} \).

This means \( \frac{1}{\log_a x} = \log_x a \). Apply this to our terms:

\[ \frac{2}{\log_a x} - \frac{1}{\log_b x} = 2\log_x a - \log_x b \]

Step 2: Apply the Power Rule

Use the logarithm power rule: \( n\log_x m = \log_x(m^n) \).

Apply this to the first term (\( 2\log_x a \)):
\[ = \log_x(a^2) - \log_x b \]

Step 3: Apply the Quotient Rule & Revert

Use the logarithm quotient rule: \( \log_x m - \log_x n = \log_x\left(\frac{m}{n}\right) \).

\[ = \log_x \left( \frac{a^2}{b} \right) \]
Finally, apply the change of base rule in reverse to match the fraction format of the options:
\[ = \frac{1}{\log_{\frac{a^2}{b}} x} \]

Therefore, Option C is correct.
