Problem

A two-digit number is formed using the digits 1, 3, 5 and 8 with no digit repeated.

The two-way table can be used to determine all possible numbers.

Three of the cells are already completed.

What is the probability that the number formed is greater than 35?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Total Outcomes

Calculate the total number of possible two-digit numbers. Since repetition is not allowed (you can't have 11, 33, 55, or 88), we ignore the diagonal of the table.

4 options for the first digit \( \times \) 3 options for the second digit.
\( 4 \times 3 = 12 \text{ total outcomes} \)

Step 2: Identify Successful Outcomes

List all the valid numbers that are strictly greater than 35.

  • Starting with 1: None (13, 15, 18 are too small)
  • Starting with 3: Only 38 (31 and 35 are not > 35)
  • Starting with 5: 51, 53, 58 (All of them)
  • Starting with 8: 81, 83, 85 (All of them)
Total successful outcomes = \( 1 + 3 + 3 = 7 \)

Step 3: Calculate Probability

Write the probability as the fraction of successful outcomes over total outcomes.

\[ P(>35) = \frac{7}{12} \]

Therefore, Option A is correct.
