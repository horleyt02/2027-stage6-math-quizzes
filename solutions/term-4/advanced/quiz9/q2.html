Problem

A triangle \( ABC \) is pictured below.

Which of the following is the correct expression for the area of \( \Delta ABC \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: State the Formula

Looking at the reference sheet, the formula for the area of a triangle is:

\[ A = \frac{1}{2}ab \sin C \]

Crucially, this formula requires two sides (\( a \) and \( b \)) and the included angle (\( C \) ) — the angle is between those two specific sides.

Step 2: Find the Missing Angle

We are given two angles (\( 100^\circ \) and \( 50^\circ \)). The sum of angles in a triangle is always \( 180^\circ \). Let's find angle \( B \):

\[ \angle B = 180^\circ - (100^\circ + 50^\circ) = 30^\circ \]

Step 3: Evaluate Side/Angle Pairs

Let's find pairs of sides and their corresponding included angles:

  • Sides 7 and 8 have the included angle \( 100^\circ \).
  • Sides 7 and 11 have the included angle \( 50^\circ \).
  • Sides 8 and 11 have the included angle \( 30^\circ \).

Step 4: Match with the Options

Looking at the options, only one correctly matches a side-side-included angle triplet:

\[ A = \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 11 \times \sin 30^\circ \]

Option C

Therefore, Option C is correct.
