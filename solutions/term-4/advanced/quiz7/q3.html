Problem

The graph of \( y = f(x) \) is shown below.

Which of the following equations best represents this graph?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Analyse the x-intercepts (Roots)

The \(x\)-intercepts dictate the factors of the polynomial equation.

At \( x = a \): The graph touches the \(x\)-axis and turns around. This indicates a repeated root (an even multiplicity), so we have a factor of \( (x - a)^2 \).

At \( x = d \): The graph cuts straight through the \(x\)-axis. This indicates a single root, so we have a factor of \( (x - d) \).

Step 2: Analyse the End Behavior

Look at the direction the graph is pointing as it moves to the right side (as \( x \to \infty \)).

The graph starts high on the left and ends low on the right. For a cubic function, this "downward" trajectory on the right side means the overall polynomial must have a negative leading coefficient.

Step 3: Construct the Equation

Combine the factors from Step 1 and apply the negative sign from Step 2:

\[ y = -(x - a)^2(x - d) \]
Distribute the negative sign into the second factor:
\[ y = (x - a)^2 [-(x - d)] \]
\[ y = (x - a)^2(d - x) \]

Option C

Therefore, Option C represents the correct function.
