Problem

Students were required to write a report of at least ten pages for an assessment.

The table below shows the discrete probability distribution for the number of pages (\( X \)) for the reports which were submitted.

All reports were at least ten pages long and the longest reports were fifteen pages.

x 10 11 12 13 14 15
\( P(X = x) \) 0.32 0.21 0.13 0.05 ? 0.03

What is the value of \( P(X = 14 \text{ or } 15) \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: All Probabilities Must Add to 1

In any complete probability distribution, the sum of all individual probabilities for all possible outcomes must always equal 1 (representing 100% of all submitted reports):

\[ \begin{gathered} P(X=10) + P(X=11) + P(X=12) + P(X=13) +\\ P(X=14) + P(X=15) = 1 \end{gathered} \]

Step 2: Find the Missing Value \( P(X = 14) \)

First, sum the five given probabilities in the table:

\[ 0.32 + 0.21 + 0.13 + 0.05 + 0.03 = \mathbf{0.74} \]

Subtract this sum from 1 to find the missing probability for 14 pages:

\[ P(X = 14) = 1 - 0.74 = \mathbf{0.26} \]
Total Probability Tape Model (Sum = 1)

Step 3: Calculate \( P(X = 14 \text{ or } 15) \)

The question asks for the probability that a report was 14 or 15 pages. Because these are mutually exclusive outcomes, we add their probabilities:

\[ P(X = 14 \text{ or } 15) = P(X = 14) + P(X = 15) \]
\[ P(X = 14 \text{ or } 15) = 0.26 + 0.03 = \mathbf{0.29} \]

0.29

Therefore, Option C is correct.
