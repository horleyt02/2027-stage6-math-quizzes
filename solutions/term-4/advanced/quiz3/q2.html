Problem

Given \( \log_a m = 0.341 \) and \( \log_a n = 3.612 \), which of the following is closest to the value of the following?

\[ \log_a \frac{m^3}{n} \]

Worked Solution

Step 1: Apply the Quotient Law

Quotient Law: \( \log_a\left(\frac{x}{y}\right) = \log_a x - \log_a y \)

We can break apart the fraction inside our logarithm into subtraction:

\[ \log_a \frac{m^3}{n} = \log_a (m^3) - \log_a n \]

Step 2: Apply the Power Law

Power Law: \( \log_a(x^k) = k \log_a x \)

Next, we can bring the exponent of 3 down to the front of its term:

\[ \mathbf{3}\log_a m - \log_a n \]

Step 3: Substitute the Given Values

We are given the numerical values for these specific logarithmic terms:

  • \( \log_a m = 0.341 \)
  • \( \log_a n = 3.612 \)

Substitute these values into our expanded expression:

\[ 3(\color{#2563eb}{0.341}) - \color{#059669}{3.612} \]

Step 4: Calculate the Final Result

Evaluate the arithmetic expression:

\[ 1.023 - 3.612 \]
\[ = -2.589 \]

\( -2.589 \)

Therefore, Option B is correct.
