Problem

The diagram shows an isosceles triangle with an area of \( 48 \text{ cm}^2 \) and equal sides of length \( x \text{ cm} \).

The angle between the equal sides is \( 36^\circ \).

NOT TO
SCALE

Which of the following expressions represents the length of the equal sides?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Use the Reference Sheet

We are given the Area, two adjacent sides, and the included angle. Look at your reference sheet for the area of a non-right-angled triangle:

\( \text{Area } = \frac{1}{2}\color{#2563eb}{(a)}\color{#059669}{(b)}\color{#dc2626}{\sin}(\color{#dc2626}{C}) \)

Step 2: Substitute the known values

Substitute the values from our specific triangle into the formula:

  • Area = \( 48 \)
  • Side \( a \) = \( x \)
  • Side \( b \) = \( x \)
  • Angle \( C \) = \( 36^\circ \)
\[ 48 = \frac{1}{2}\color{#2563eb}{(x)}\color{#059669}{(x)}\color{#dc2626}{\sin}(\color{#dc2626}{36^\circ}) \]

Step 3: Simplify and Rearrange

Combine the \( x \) terms (\( x \times x = x^2 \)):

\[ 48 = \frac{1}{2} x^2 \sin(36^\circ) \]
To match the options, remove the fraction by multiplying both sides by 2:
\[ 48 \times 2 = x^2 \sin(36^\circ) \]
\[ 96 = x^2 \sin(36^\circ) \]

Option C

Therefore, Option C is correct.
