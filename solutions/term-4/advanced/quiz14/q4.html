Problem

The graph of a function \( y = f(x) \) is shown below.

Which of the following best represents the equation of the function?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Use the Vertical Asymptote to Set Up the Form

The basic logarithm \( y = \ln x \) has a vertical asymptote at \( x = 0 \). Because this graph has a vertical asymptote at \( x = 1 \) and exists for \( x > 1 \), it has been shifted \( 1 \) unit to the right:

\[ x - 1 = 0 \implies x = 1 \quad (\text{vertical asymptote}) \]
\[ y = k\ln(x - 1) \]

Step 2: Substitute the Point \( (e + 1, 2) \) to Find the Scale Factor \( k \)

Substitute \( x = e + 1 \) and \( y = 2 \) into \( y = k\ln(x - 1) \), recalling that \( \ln(e) = 1 \):

\[ 2 = k\ln\big((e + 1) - 1\big) \]
\[ 2 = k\ln(e) \]
\[ 2 = k(1) \implies k = 2 \]
\[ y = 2\ln(x - 1) \]

Step 3: Visualise the Vertical Stretch by a Factor of \( 2 \)

Multiplying \( \ln(x - 1) \) by \( 2 \) outside the logarithm keeps the \( x \)-intercept fixed at \( (2, 0) \) while doubling every \( y \)-value — lifting \( (e + 1, 1) \) directly up to \( (e + 1, 2) \):

\( y = \ln(x - 1) \) \( y = 2\ln(x - 1) \) Asymptote \( x = 1 \)

Therefore, Option C is the correct choice.
