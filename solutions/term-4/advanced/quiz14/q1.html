Problem

Below are the cumulative frequency histogram and polygon (ogive) showing the scores of \( 100 \) students.

Based on this graph, what is the median test score?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Find the Median Position on the Vertical Axis

The median is the \( 50\text{th} \) percentile — the test score at which half of the total number of students sit below and half sit above. Since there are \( N = 100 \) students in total:

\[ \text{Median position} = 50\% \text{ of } 100 = \frac{100}{2} = 50 \]
Start at Cumulative Frequency \( = 50 \) on the vertical (\( y \)) axis.

Step 2: Trace Across to the Ogive and Down to the Test Score

Draw a horizontal line from \( \text{CF} = 50 \) across until it meets the red ogive curve, which hits the exact plotted point \( (60, 50) \). Then drop vertically down to the horizontal axis:

Median trace (\( \text{CF} = 50 \to \text{Score} = 60 \)) Intersection \( (60, 50) \)

Step 3: State the Median Test Score

The vertical drop line lands directly on \( 60 \) on the Test Score axis, meaning \( 50 \) students scored \( 60 \) or below and \( 50 \) students scored above \( 60 \):

\[ \text{Median Test Score} = 60 \]

Therefore, Option D is the correct choice.
