Problem

Consider the diagram shown.

What is the area of the shaded segment?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Identify the strategy

The shaded region is a major segment. We can calculate its area by adding the area of the major sector to the area of the triangle.

Area = Area of Major Sector + Area of Triangle

Step 2: Find the angle of the major sector

A full circle is \( 2\pi \) radians. Subtract the minor sector angle to find the major sector angle (\( \theta \)):

\(\theta = 2\pi - \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{5\pi}{3}\)

Step 3: Calculate the area of the major sector

Using the formula on the reference sheet \( A = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta \):

\( \text{Sector Area} = \frac{1}{2}(12)^2\left(\frac{5\pi}{3}\right) \)

\( \text{Sector Area} = \frac{1}{2}(144)\left(\frac{5\pi}{3}\right) \)

\( \text{Sector Area} = 72\left(\frac{5\pi}{3}\right) = 120\pi \)

Step 4: Calculate the area of the triangle

Using the formula on the reference sheet \( A = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin(C) \):

\( \text{Triangle Area} = \frac{1}{2}(12)(12)\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{3}\right) \)

\( \text{Triangle Area} = 72\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) \)

\( \text{Triangle Area} = 36\sqrt{3} \)

Step 5: Combine for final area

Add the two areas together:

\( \text{Total Area} = 120\pi + 36\sqrt{3} \text{ cm}^2 \)

Therefore, Option A is correct.

Alternative Method: You could also solve this by finding the area of the entire circle (\( 144\pi \)) and subtracting the area of the unshaded minor segment (\( 24\pi - 36\sqrt{3} \)).
