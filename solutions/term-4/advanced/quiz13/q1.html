Problem

What is the area of a sector of a circle of radius \( 6\text{ cm} \) that contains an angle of \( \dfrac{\pi}{6} \) radians at the centre of the circle?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Visualise the Sector and Identify Given Values

A full circle contains \( 2\pi \) radians, so a central angle of \( \theta = \frac{\pi}{6} \) radians (\( 30^\circ \)) is exactly \( \frac{1}{12} \) of the full circle:

Shaded Sector (\( \frac{1}{12} \) of circle) Radius (\( r = 6\text{ cm} \)

Step 2: Recall the Area of a Sector Formula (in Radians)

From the reference sheet, when the angle \( \theta \) is measured in radians, the area of a sector is:

\[ A = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta \]
Substitute \( r = 6 \) and \( \theta = \dfrac{\pi}{6} \):
\[ A = \frac{1}{2}(6)^2\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right) \]

Step 3: Simplify to Find the Exact Area

Evaluate the expression step by step:

\[ A = \frac{1}{2} \times 36 \times \frac{\pi}{6} \]
\[ A = 18 \times \frac{\pi}{6} \]
\[ A = 3\pi\text{ cm}^2 \]

Sense Check: The full circle's area is \( \pi r^2 = 36\pi\text{ cm}^2 \), and \( \frac{1}{12} \) of \( 36\pi \) is indeed \( 3\pi\text{ cm}^2 \).

Therefore, Option B is the correct choice.
