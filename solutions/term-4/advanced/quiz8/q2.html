Problem

Consider \( g(x) = 3x - 1 \).

What is the value of \( g(g(2)) \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Understand Composite Functions

A composite function like \( g(g(2)) \) means we need to evaluate the function from the inside out.

First, we must find the value of the inner expression: \( g(2) \).

Step 2: Evaluate the Inner Function

Substitute \( x = 2 \) into the given function equation \( g(x) = 3x - 1 \).

\[ g(2) = 3(2) - 1 \]
\[ g(2) = 6 - 1 \]
\[ g(2) = 5 \]

Now we know that the "inside" of our composite function equals 5.

Step 3: Evaluate the Outer Function

Substitute the result from Step 2 back into the outer function. We are now evaluating \( g(5) \).

\[ g(5) = 3(5) - 1 \]
\[ g(5) = 15 - 1 \]
