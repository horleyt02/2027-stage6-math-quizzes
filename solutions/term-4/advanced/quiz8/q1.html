Problem

What is the exact value of the following expression?

\[ \cos 225^\circ + \sec 60^\circ \]

Worked Solution

Step 1: Evaluate \( \cos 225^\circ \)

First, find the reference angle for \( 225^\circ \). Since it is in the 3rd quadrant, the reference angle is \( 225^\circ - 180^\circ = \mathbf{45^\circ} \).

According to the ASTC rule ("All Stations To Central"), only Tangent is positive in the 3rd quadrant. Therefore, Cosine must be negative.

\[ \cos 225^\circ = -\cos 45^\circ = \mathbf{-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}} \]

Step 2: Evaluate \( \sec 60^\circ \)

Recall that secant is the reciprocal of cosine: \( \sec \theta = \frac{1}{\cos \theta} \).

We know from exact value triangles that \( \cos 60^\circ = \frac{1}{2} \).

Therefore, the reciprocal is:

\[ \sec 60^\circ = \frac{2}{1} = \mathbf{2} \]

Step 3: Combine with a Common Denominator

Now substitute both evaluated parts back into the original expression and add them together.

\[ -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} + 2 \]
Multiply the 2 by \( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}} \) to get a common denominator:
\[ -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} + \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}} \]

\[ \frac{2\sqrt{2} - 1}{\sqrt{2}} \]

Therefore, Option B is correct.
