Problem

It is known for the angle \( \varphi \) that

\[ \tan(\varphi) = \frac{12}{5} \quad \text{and} \quad \cos(\varphi) = -\frac{5}{13} \]

Which of the following graphs best represents the angle \( \varphi \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Check the Sign of \(\tan(\varphi)\)

We are given that:

\[ \tan(\varphi) = \frac{12}{5} > 0 \]

Tangent is positive in Quadrant 1 and in Quadrant 3.

Step 2: Check the Sign of \(\cos(\varphi)\)

We are also given that:

\[ \cos(\varphi) = -\frac{5}{13} < 0 \]

Cosine is negative in Quadrant 2 and Quadrant 3.

Step 3: Combine with the ASTC Diagram

To satisfy both conditions simultaneously, the angle must lie in the quadrant where both conditions overlap:

ASTC: All Stations To Central
\[ \tan(\varphi) > 0 \implies \text{Quadrant 1 or 3} \]
\[ \cos(\varphi) < 0 \implies \text{Quadrant 2 or 3} \]
\[ \text{Overlap} = \mathbf{\text{Quadrant 3}} \]

Graph C (Quadrant 3)

Therefore, Graph C correctly shows the ray in Quadrant 3 measured anticlockwise from the positive \(x\)-axis.
