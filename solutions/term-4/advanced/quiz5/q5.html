Problem

\( A \) and \( B \) are two events such that

\[ P(A) = \frac{2}{5}, \quad P(B) = \frac{3}{10} \quad \text{and} \quad P(A \cup B) = \frac{1}{2} \]

What is the value of \( P(B|A) \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Find the Intersection \( P(A \cap B) \)

From the reference sheet, the addition rule relates the union and intersection:

From the Reference Sheet:
\[ P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B) \]

Convert all probabilities to a common denominator of 10:

\[ P(A) = \frac{4}{10}, \quad P(B) = \frac{3}{10}, \quad P(A \cup B) = \frac{5}{10} \]

Rearranging to solve for \( P(A \cap B) \):

\[ P(A \cap B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cup B) \]
\[ P(A \cap B) = \frac{4}{10} + \frac{3}{10} - \frac{5}{10} = \mathbf{\frac{2}{10}} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{5}} \]

Step 2: Adapt the Conditional Probability Formula

The reference sheet lists the conditional probability formula given event \( B \):

From the Reference Sheet:
\[ P(A|B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)} \]

We need \( P(B|A) \), meaning given event \( A \) has occurred. The condition \( A \) becomes the denominator:

\[ P(B|A) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(A)} \]

Step 3: Evaluate

The condition 'given \( A \)' restricts our sample space strictly to the total probability of \( A \), that is \( \frac{4}{10} \):

\[ P(B|A) = \frac{\frac{2}{10}}{\frac{4}{10}} = \frac{2}{4} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{2}} \]

\( \frac{1}{2} \)

Therefore, Option A is correct.
