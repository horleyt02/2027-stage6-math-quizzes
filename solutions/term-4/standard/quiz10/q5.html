Problem

A collection of bushwalking tracks is modelled by the network below.

There are six checkpoints labelled \( A, B, C, D, E \) and \( F \).

Which of the following two edges should be removed so that a bushwalker could walk along every track exactly once?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Define Traversability

To walk every track exactly once, the network must have an Eulerian trail or Eulerian circuit.

  • Rule: A connected network is traversable if it has exactly 0 or 2 vertices with an odd degree.
  • The degree is the number of edges connected to a vertex.

Step 2: Analyse the Original Graph

Count the degrees of every vertex in the current graph.

A: 3
B: 4
C: 4
D: 2
E: 3
F: 4

While it currently has 2 odd vertices, the question asks us to create a new valid network by removing two edges.

Step 3: Test Option A

Remove edges \( AB \) and \( BE \). How does this affect the degrees?

  • Removing \( AB \) drops the degree of A by 1 and B by 1.
  • Removing \( BE \) drops the degree of B by 1 and E by 1.
A: 2
B: 2
C: 4
D: 2
E: 2
F: 4

All vertices now have an even degree (0 odd vertices). This creates a valid Eulerian circuit!

Therefore, Option A is correct.
