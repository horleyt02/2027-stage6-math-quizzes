Problem

Two water bottles have an average capacity of 1.35 L.

If one of the bottles holds 850 mL, what is the capacity of the other bottle?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Convert to the same units

The problem uses both Litres (L) and millilitres (mL). Let's convert everything to Litres.

\( 850 \text{ mL} \div 1000 = 0.85 \text{ L} \)

Step 2: Find the Total Capacity

We are given the average of two bottles. To find the sum of their capacities, we must multiply the average by 2.

Total Capacity = Average \( \times \) Number of items
\( 1.35 \text{ L} \times 2 = 2.70 \text{ L} \)

Step 3: Subtract to find the unknown

Now that we know the total capacity of both bottles, subtract the known bottle's capacity to find the unknown one.

\[ 2.70 \text{ L} - 0.85 \text{ L} = 1.85 \text{ L} \]

1.85 L

Therefore, Option C is correct.
