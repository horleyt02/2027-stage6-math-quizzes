Problem

Two relationships between variables are represented below, one by a graph and the other by an equation.

The relationship between \( M \) and \( N \)

The relationship between \( P \) and \( Q \)

\[ P = 4.5Q \]

Which relationship is an example of direct variation?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Define Direct Variation

Direct variation occurs when two variables are directly proportional to each other. This means they increase or decrease at a constant rate together.

  • Algebraically: It must take the form \( y = kx \) (where \( k \) is a constant, and there is no \(+c\) or \(y\)-intercept).
  • Graphically: It must be a straight line that passes exactly through the origin (0,0).

Step 2: Analyse the Graph (M and N)

Look at the graph representing the relationship between \( M \) and \( N \).

It is a straight line, and it starts exactly at the origin \( (0,0) \).

Therefore, the relationship between \( M \) and \( N \) is an example of direct variation.

Step 3: Analyse the Equation (P and Q)

Look at the equation representing the relationship between \( P \) and \( Q \): \( P = 4.5Q \).

This perfectly matches the form \( y = kx \). The constant of variation \( k \) is \( 4.5 \), and there is no extra number added or subtracted at the end (the y-intercept is 0).

Therefore, the relationship between \( P \) and \( Q \) is also an example of direct variation.

Both relationships

Therefore, Option A is correct.
