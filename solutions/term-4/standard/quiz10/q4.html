Problem

Chloe and Dylan both shop at a clearance sale at a sporting goods store.

Chloe buys a running watch marked at $260 for a discounted price of $208.

Dylan buys a gym bag marked at $80 and gets a discount of $14.40 off this price.

Who received the greater percentage discount, and by how much?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Chloe's Discount Percentage

First, find the dollar amount of Chloe's discount, then express it as a percentage of the original price.

  • Discount amount: $260 − $208 = $52
  • Percentage of original: \( \frac{52}{260} \times 100\% = 20\% \)

Step 2: Dylan's Discount Percentage

We are already given Dylan's dollar discount ($14.40). Express it as a percentage of the original price.

Percentage of original:
\( \frac{14.40}{80} \times 100\% = 18\% \)

Step 3: Compare the Percentages

Compare the two percentages to find who had the larger discount and by how much.

Chloe (20%) vs Dylan (18%)
Difference: 20% − 18% = 2%

Chloe received a greater percentage discount by 2%.

Therefore, Option A is correct.
