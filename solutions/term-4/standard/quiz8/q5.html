Problem

This open crate has length \(4a\), width \(2a\) and height \(b\).

Which of the following expressions gives the total surface area of the open crate?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Unfold into a Net

To find the surface area, we need to calculate the area of all the individual faces. Because the crate is open, there is no top face. Let's unfold the 3D box into a flat 2D net.

Hover or tap the faces below to identify them!

Step 2: Calculate Face Areas

Calculate the area (\( \text{length} \times \text{width} \)) for each distinct type of face:

1 x Base \( 4a \times 2a = \mathbf{8a^2} \)
2 x Front/Back \( 2 \times (4a \times b) = \mathbf{8ab} \)
2 x Left/Right Sides \( 2 \times (2a \times b) = \mathbf{4ab} \)

Step 3: Total Surface Area

Add all the areas together to find the total surface area:

\[ \text{SA} = 8a^2 + 8ab + 4ab \]
Combine like terms (the 'ab' terms):
\[ \text{SA} = 8a^2 + 12ab \]

Option C

Therefore, Option C is correct.
