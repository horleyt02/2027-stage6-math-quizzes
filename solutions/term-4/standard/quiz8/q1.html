Each week, Jacob wants to allocate 20% of his weekly wage to savings.

What adjustment will Jacob need to make to his budget?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Current Savings

First, we need to find out how much Jacob is currently saving. We subtract all his listed expenses from his total wage.

\[ \text{Total Exp} = \$440 + \$75 + \$55 + \$200 + \$250 = \$1020 \]
\[ \text{Current Savings} = \$1255 - \$1020 = \mathbf{\$235} \]

Step 2: Target Savings

Jacob wants to save exactly 20% of his weekly wage ($1255).

\[ 0.20 \times \$1255 = \mathbf{\$251} \]

Step 3: The Shortfall

How much extra money does he need to find to reach his target?

\[ \$251 - \$235 = \mathbf{\$16} \]

He needs to cut $16 from his expenses.

Step 4: Percentage Adjustment

The options suggest cutting this $16 from his "Personal expenses". We must calculate $16 as a percentage of his current Personal expenses budget ($200).

6 Personal expenses $200
\[ \frac{16}{200} \times 100 = \mathbf{8\%} \]

Option A

Therefore, Option A is correct.
