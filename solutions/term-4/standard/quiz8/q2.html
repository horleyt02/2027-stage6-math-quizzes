Problem

The results of a group of 50 students involved in a reading program are displayed below in a parallel box plot. The data collected shows the reading speed in words per minute of the students both before and after the administration of the program.

Based on the parallel box plot, which statement accurately describes the reading speeds of the 50 students?

Worked Solution

Let's evaluate each option systematically by reading the box plots.

Evaluating Option A (Range)

Range is calculated as Maximum - Minimum.

  • Before Range: \( 95 - 62 = 33 \)
  • After Range: \( 96 - 64 = 32 \)
The range decreased by 1, it did not increase. Option A is False.

Evaluating Option C (IQR)

Interquartile Range (IQR) is calculated as Q3 - Q1 (the width of the box).

  • Before IQR: \( 80 - 68 = 12 \)
  • After IQR: \( 90 - 74 = 16 \)
The IQR increased from 12 to 16. The statement has it backwards. Option C is False.

Evaluating Option D (Skewness)

Skewness is determined by the direction of the longer "tail" (whisker and box half).

Looking at the 'Before' plot, the right side (from the median 72 up to 95) is much more stretched out than the left side (from 62 to 72). Because the tail extends to the right (positive direction), it is positively skewed.

The distribution is positively skewed, not negatively. Option D is False.

Evaluating Option B (Median)

The central line in the box represents the Median, which marks the 50th percentile. This means exactly 50% of the data lies at or above this value.

  • Looking at the 'After' plot, the median line is at exactly 82 words per minute.
  • Because there are 50 students in total, 50% of them is \( 50 \times 0.50 = \mathbf{25 \text{ students}} \).
Therefore, 25 students (the top 50%) read at 82 wpm or faster. Option B is True.

Option B
