Problem

Marcus filled the petrol tank in his car with 52 litres of petrol.

The following diagram shows the information displayed on the petrol bowser.

Marcus paid $90.95 for the petrol.

What type of petrol did Marcus purchase?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Calculate the price per litre (in dollars)

To find the unit price, we divide the total cost by the total number of litres purchased.

\[ \$90.95 \div 52 \text{ L} \approx \$1.749 \text{ per litre} \]

Step 2: Convert dollars to cents

The bowser displays the fuel prices in cents per litre. We need to convert our dollar value into cents by multiplying by 100.

\[ \$1.749 \times 100 = \mathbf{174.9 \text{ cents per litre}} \]

Step 3: Match with the bowser

Looking at the values on the petrol bowser diagram:

  • Unleaded 98: 182.9
  • Unleaded 95: 174.9
  • Unleaded E10: 165.9
  • Diesel: 169.9

Unleaded 95

Therefore, Option B is correct.
