Problem

The diagram shows the dimensions of a rectangular prism.

The prism has a capacity of 40.5 litres.

What is the height (\( h \)) of the prism?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Convert Capacity to Volume

We are given the capacity in litres (L), but the dimensions of the prism are in centimetres (cm). We need to convert the capacity into a volume of cubic centimetres (\(\text{cm}^3\)).

From your Reference Sheet:
  • \( 1 \text{ cm}^3 = 1 \text{ mL} \)
  • \( 1 \text{ m}^3 = 1000 \text{ L} \) (Not needed here, but good to know!)
Since we know there are \( 1000 \text{ mL} \) in \( 1 \text{ L} \), the reference sheet confirms that \( \mathbf{1 \text{ L} = 1000 \text{ cm}^3} \).

Let's convert our specific capacity:

\[ \text{Volume (V)} = 40.5 \times 1000 = \mathbf{40,500 \text{ cm}^3} \]

Step 2: Find the Area of the Base (\(A\))

The volume of any uniform prism can be found using the formula \( V = Ah \), where \( A \) is the area of the base.

First, let's calculate the area of the rectangular base using the given dimensions:

\[ A = 90 \times 30 = \mathbf{2700 \text{ cm}^2} \]

Step 3: Substitute into the Volume Formula

Now, substitute the volume (\(V\)) from Step 1 and the base area (\(A\)) from Step 2 into the prism volume formula:

\[ V = Ah \]
\[ 40,500 = 2700 \times h \]

Step 4: Solve for Height (\(h\) )

Divide both sides of the equation by 2700 to isolate the height \( h \):

\[ h = \frac{40,500}{2700} \] \[ h = 15 \text{ cm} \]

15 cm

Therefore, Option C is correct.
