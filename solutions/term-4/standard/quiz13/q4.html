Problem

A city has the coordinates \( (45^\circ\text{N}, \; 120^\circ\text{W}) \).

What is the Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) of this city?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Identify the Longitude (Ignore Latitude)

Time zones are determined solely by longitude (East/West), not latitude (North/South). Separate the given coordinates \( (45^\circ\text{N}, \; 120^\circ\text{W}) \):

Not Used for Time
\( 45^\circ\text{N} \)
Latitude (North–South)
Use This Value
\( 120^\circ\text{W} \)
Longitude (East–West)

Step 2: Calculate the Time Difference from the Reference Sheet

From the Time and location section of the reference sheet:

\[ 15^\circ = 1\text{ hour time difference} \]
Divide the longitude \( 120^\circ \) by \( 15^\circ \):
\[ \text{Time difference} = \frac{120^\circ}{15^\circ} = 8\text{ hours} \]

Step 3: Determine the Sign (East = Ahead, West = Behind)

Locations East of the Prime Meridian (\( 0^\circ \)) are ahead of UTC (\( + \)), while locations West are behind UTC (\( - \)). Since \( 120^\circ\text{W} \) is West of the Prime Meridian:

\( 120^\circ\text{W} \to \text{UTC} -8 \) Prime Meridian (\( 0^\circ \))
\[ 120^\circ\text{W} \implies \text{UTC} -8 \]

Therefore, Option C is the correct choice.
