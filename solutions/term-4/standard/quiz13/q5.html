Problem

Sarah recorded the number of goals that she scored over a series of games.

She recorded her results in the frequency histogram shown. She did not record the result for seven goals scored.

The mean number of goals scored was \( 5.4 \).

In how many games did Sarah score seven goals?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Organise the Histogram into a Frequency Table

Let \( n \) be the missing number of games in which Sarah scored \( 7 \) goals. Read each bar height \( f \) and calculate \( fx \):

Goals (\( x \)) Games (\( f \)) Total Goals (\( fx \))
\( 3 \)\( 3 \)\( 3 \times 3 = 9 \)
\( 4 \)\( 7 \)\( 4 \times 7 = 28 \)
\( 5 \)\( 5 \)\( 5 \times 5 = 25 \)
\( 6 \)\( 1 \)\( 6 \times 1 = 6 \)
\( 7 \)\( n \)\( 7n \)
\( 8 \)\( 4 \)\( 8 \times 4 = 32 \)
Total \( \sum f = 20 + n \) \( \sum fx = 100 + 7n \)

Step 2: Use the Mean Formula to Solve for \( n \)

Substitute \( \bar{x} = 5.4 \), \( \sum fx = 100 + 7n \), and \( \sum f = 20 + n \) into \( \bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum fx}{\sum f} \):

\[ \frac{100 + 7n}{20 + n} = 5.4 \]
\[ 100 + 7n = 5.4(20 + n) \]
\[ 100 + 7n = 108 + 5.4n \]
\[ 7n - 5.4n = 108 - 100 \implies 1.6n = 8 \]
\[ n = \frac{8}{1.6} = 5 \]

Step 3: Complete the Histogram and Verify the Mean

Filling in the missing bar at \( 7 \) goals with a height of \( 5 \) games balances the distribution at the exact mean \( \bar{x} = 5.4 \):

Filled Bar (\( 7 \text{ goals} \to 5 \text{ games} \)) Mean (\( \bar{x} = 5.4 \))
Verification check:
\[ \bar{x} = \frac{100 + 7(5)}{20 + 5} = \frac{135}{25} = 5.4 \;\checkmark \]

Therefore, Sarah scored seven goals in five games (Option D).
