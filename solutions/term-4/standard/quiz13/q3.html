Problem

Consider the triangular prism below.

What is the volume of the triangular prism?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Identify the Cross-Section and Ignore the Distractor

The uniform cross-section of this prism is a right-angled triangle with perpendicular base \( b = 15\text{ m} \) and height \( h = 8\text{ m} \). The slanted edge of \( 17\text{ m} \) is the hypotenuse and is not used to find the area:

Cross-section (\( A = 60\text{ m}^2 \)) Depth (\( l = 6\text{ m} \)) Distractor (\( 17\text{ m} \))

Step 2: Calculate the Area of the Triangular Cross-Section

Use the perpendicular sides \( b = 15\text{ m} \) and \( h = 8\text{ m} \) in the area of a triangle formula \( A = \frac{1}{2}bh \):

\[ A = \frac{1}{2} \times b \times h = \frac{1}{2} \times 15 \times 8 \]
\[ A = 60\text{ m}^2 \]

Step 3: Multiply by the Prism Length (Depth) to Find Volume

The volume of any prism is given by \( V = A \times l \), where \( l = 6\text{ m} \) is the length/depth of the prism:

\[ V = A \times l = 60 \times 6 \]
\[ V = 360\text{ m}^3 \]

Therefore, Option A is the correct choice.
