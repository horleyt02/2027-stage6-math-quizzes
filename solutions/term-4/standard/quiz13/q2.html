Problem

Maya received a pay rise and now makes \( \$70\,200 \) per annum.

Her salary before receiving the pay rise was \( \$65\,000 \).

What was the percentage increase of her salary?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Find the Dollar Amount of the Pay Rise

Subtract Maya's original salary from her new salary to find the actual increase:

\[ \text{Increase} = \$70\,200 - \$65\,000 = \$5\,200 \]
Original Base (\( \$65\,000 = 100\% \)) Pay Rise (\( +\$5\,200 \))

Step 2: Divide the Increase by the Original Salary

A percentage increase is always measured relative to the original (before) value, not the new value:

\[ \text{Percentage Increase} = \frac{\text{Increase}}{\text{Original Salary}} \times 100\% \]
\[ = \frac{5\,200}{65\,000} \times 100\% \]
\[ = 0.08 \times 100\% \]

Step 3: Multiply by \( 100 \) to Express as a Percentage

Multiplying the decimal \( 0.08 \) by \( 100 \) converts it into a percentage:

\[ 0.08 \times 100\% = 8\% \]

Quick Check: \( \$65\,000 \times 1.08 = \$70\,200 \;\checkmark \).

Therefore, Option D is the correct choice.
