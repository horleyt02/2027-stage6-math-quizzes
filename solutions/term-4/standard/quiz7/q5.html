Problem

Consider location \( A \) on the globe below.

What are the coordinates for location \( A \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Identify the Latitude

Latitude lines are the horizontal lines (parallels) indicating distance North or South of the equator.

Locate the horizontal grid line that passes directly through location \( A \). Following this line to the right edge of the globe, it is explicitly labeled as \( 30^\circ\text{N} \).

Step 2: Interpolate the Longitude

Longitude lines are the vertical, curved lines (meridians) indicating distance East or West of the Prime Meridian.

Location \( A \) does not sit exactly on a labeled longitude line. Instead, it is situated exactly halfway between the line labeled \( 60^\circ\text{W} \) and the line labeled \( 90^\circ\text{W} \).

To find the exact longitude, calculate the midpoint:

\[ \frac{60 + 90}{2} = \mathbf{75^\circ\text{W}} \]

Step 3: Combine with Standard Convention

Important Rule:

Geographic coordinates must always be written in the standard order of (Latitude, Longitude).

Combining our findings in the correct order gives:

\[ (\color{#2563eb}{30^\circ\text{N}}, \color{#db2777}{75^\circ\text{W}}) \]

Option C

Therefore, Option C is correct.
