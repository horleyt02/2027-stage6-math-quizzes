Problem

Which of the following triangles is right-angled?

Worked Solution

Step 1: State the Rule

The converse of the Pythagorean theorem states that a triangle is right-angled if and only if the square of its longest side (hypotenuse) equals the sum of the squares of the two shorter sides.

\[ a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \]

Step 2: Test Option B

Let's test the side lengths from Option B: \( 8 \), \( 15 \), and \( 17 \). Here, \( 17 \) is the longest side, so it must be \( c \).

Sum of squares of shorter sides:
\[ 8^2 + 15^2 = 64 + 225 = \mathbf{289} \]
Square of longest side:
\[ 17^2 = \mathbf{289} \]

Step 3: Conclude

Compare the two values calculated in Step 2.

\[ 289 = 289 \]

Since the equation holds perfectly true, the triangle with sides 8, 15, and 17 satisfies the Pythagorean theorem.

Triangle B is right-angled.

Therefore, Option B is correct.
