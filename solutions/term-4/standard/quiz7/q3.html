Which of these statements is true in comparing the two groups?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Count Members & Find Ranges

Let's systematically check Options A and D by finding the total count and range (Max - Min) for both groups.

Group 1:
  • Count: Count the leaves on the left. There are exactly 20 members.
  • Range: Max (97) - Min (43) = 54.
Group 2:
  • Count: Count the leaves on the right. There are 25 members.
  • Range: Max (96) - Min (40) = 56.

Since 20 < 25, Option A is false.
Since 54 < 56, Option D is false.

Step 2: Calculate the Medians

Let's check Option B. The median is the exact middle value of an ordered dataset.

Group 1 Median (20 members):

The middle falls between the 10th and 11th values. Counting from smallest (43) upwards, the 10th value is 65 and the 11th is 66.
Median = \( \frac{65 + 66}{2} \) = 65.5

Group 2 Median (25 members):

The middle is exactly the 13th value. Counting from smallest (40) upwards, the 13th value is 66.

Since 65.5 < 66, Group 1 does not have the greater median. Option B is false.

Step 3: Find the Modes

This leaves Option C. The mode is the value that appears most frequently.

Group 1 Mode: Looking at the leaves, the digit '3' appears twice in the '6' stem. The mode is 63.

Group 2 Mode: The digit '2' appears three times in the '5' stem. The mode is 52.

63 > 52

Group 1 indeed has the greater mode. Option C is correct.
