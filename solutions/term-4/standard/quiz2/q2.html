Problem

On Sunday, Mickey loads $20 onto a new public transport pass. During the week (Monday to Friday), he takes the bus to and from his friend's house. Each bus ride costs $1.50.

Which graph shows the balance on Mickey's public transport pass during the week?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Determine the Direction (Gradient)

Mickey starts with $20 (the \( y \)-intercept). Because he is spending money on bus rides, his balance must decrease over time.

This means the line must slope downwards. We can immediately eliminate Options C and D.

Step 2: Calculate the Daily Cost

The problem states he travels to and from his friend's house. This means he takes 2 bus rides per day.

Daily Cost = 2 rides \( \times \) $1.50 = $3.00 per day

This tells us the gradient (slope) of our line is -3.

Step 3: Calculate the Final Balance

Let's calculate his balance at the end of the 5-day week to see which graph matches.

  • Total spent: 5 days \( \times \) $3.00/day = $15.00
  • Final balance: $20.00 initial - $15.00 spent = $5.00

Step 4: Verify the Graph

We are looking for the downward-sloping graph that ends at exactly $5 on day 5 (coordinate point \( (5, 5) \)).

  • Graph A ends at $12.50.
  • Graph B ends at exactly $5.

Graph B

Therefore, Option B is correct.
