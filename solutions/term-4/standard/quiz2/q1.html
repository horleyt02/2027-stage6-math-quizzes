Problem

In this network diagram, there is a loop at vertex \( P \).

What is the degree of vertex \( P \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Define "Degree"

The degree of a vertex in a network is simply the number of line ends that touch (or are incident to) that vertex.

Step 2: The "Circle Cut" Visual Method

To make counting easier and avoid missing lines, imagine drawing a small circle tightly around vertex \( P \). Every time a line crosses this circle, it adds 1 to the degree.

Step 3: Count the components

  • There are 4 straight lines connecting \( P \) to the other vertices (\( Q, U, T, S \)).
  • The loop starts and ends at \( P \), meaning it crosses our boundary circle twice. A loop always contributes 2 to the degree.

Step 4: Calculate total

Add the components together:

4 + 2 = 6

Degree = 6

Therefore, Option C is correct.
