Problem

How many cubic centimetres are contained in a cubic metre?

Worked Solution

Visualizing the Cube

Algebraic Substitution

The volume of a cube is calculated by multiplying its length, width, and height. By substituting \( 1 \text{ m} \) with \( 100 \text{ cm} \) for each dimension, we can calculate the total cubic centimetres:

\[ 1 \text{ m}^3 = 1 \text{ m} \times 1 \text{ m} \times 1 \text{ m} \] \[ \rightarrow 100 \text{ cm} \times 100 \text{ cm} \times 100 \text{ cm} \] \[ = 1,000,000 \text{ cm}^3 \] \[ = 10^6 \text{ cm}^3 \]

\( 10^6 \)

Therefore, Option C is correct.
