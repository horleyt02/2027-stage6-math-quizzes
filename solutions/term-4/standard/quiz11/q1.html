Problem

The coordinates of Nairobi are \( (1^\circ\text{S}, 37^\circ\text{E}) \).

A town is located \( 8^\circ \) north and \( 15^\circ \) west of Nairobi.

What are the coordinates of the town?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Calculate Latitude (North/South)

Start at \( 1^\circ\text{S} \) (which is 1 degree below the equator). Moving \( 8^\circ \) North means you are heading towards and across the equator (\( 0^\circ \)).

  • It takes \( 1^\circ \) North to reach the equator.
  • You have \( 7^\circ \) left to travel (\( 8 - 1 = 7 \)).
New Latitude: \( 7^\circ\text{N} \)

Step 2: Calculate Longitude (East/West)

Start at \( 37^\circ\text{E} \). Moving \( 15^\circ \) West means you are heading towards the Prime Meridian (\( 0^\circ \)).

Because you are moving back towards 0 from a positive Eastern longitude, you subtract the movement:

\( 37^\circ\text{E} - 15^\circ = 22^\circ\text{E} \)

Step 3: Combine Coordinates

Combine the new latitude and the new longitude to get the final coordinate location of the town.

\( (7^\circ\text{N}, 22^\circ\text{E}) \)

Therefore, Option C is correct.
