Problem

Six students go bowling together.

Their scores at the end of the first game are shown below.

81
90
50
89
88
81

One of the students' scores is an outlier.

Which statistical measure will NOT change if the outlier is removed from the data?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Identify the Outlier and Sort

First, sort the scores in ascending order to easily see the spread of the data.

50, 81, 81, 88, 89, 90
The score of 50 is significantly lower than the rest of the cluster (80s-90s), so it is the outlier.

Step 2: Analyse "Before" and "After"

Let's evaluate what happens to each measure conceptually if we remove the 50.

Mean: It changes (increases). The low score of 50 was dragging the team average down. Removing it raises the average of the remaining higher scores.
Median: It changes. The original median was 84.5 (average of the middle two: 81 and 88). Without 50, there are 5 numbers, and the new exact middle is 88.
Range: It changes (decreases). The original range was \( 90 - 50 = 40 \). The new range is \( 90 - 81 = 9 \).
Mode: It stays the same. The most frequent score was 81 before, and it is still 81 after removing the 50.

Step 3: Conclusion

Based on our analysis, only one statistical measure remains unaffected by the removal of the outlier.

The mode

Therefore, Option C is correct.
