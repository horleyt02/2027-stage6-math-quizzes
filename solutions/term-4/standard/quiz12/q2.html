Problem

A network graph is shown.

How many edges will a spanning tree have?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Define Spanning Tree Rule

A spanning tree is a subgraph that connects all the vertices of a network without containing any cycles (loops).

Fundamental Rule: For any connected network with \( n \) vertices, a spanning tree must contain exactly \( n - 1 \) edges.

Step 2: Count the Vertices

Look at the original graph and count the number of nodes (vertices).

The vertices are \( A, B, C, D, E, \) and \( F \).
There are \( 6 \) vertices in total (\( n = 6 \)).

Step 3: Calculate the Edges

Apply the spanning tree formula using our vertex count.

\( \text{Edges} = n - 1 \)
\( \text{Edges} = 6 - 1 = 5 \)

Therefore, Option B is correct.
