Problem

Consider the following line. Which equation best represents the line?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Identify the y-intercept (\( c \))

First, find where the line crosses the vertical \( y \)-axis. This point is called the y-intercept.

The line crosses the axis at \( (0, -6) \). Therefore, the y-intercept is:

\( c = -6 \)

Step 2: Calculate the Gradient (\( m \))

Next, we pick a second clear point on the grid, such as \( (9, -3) \), to find the gradient (\( m = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} \)).

\[ m = \frac{\text{Rise}}{\text{Run}} \]
\[ m = \frac{+3}{+9} = \frac{1}{3} \]

Step 3: Combine into the Equation

The general form of a straight line is \( y = mx + c \). Substitute the values we found:

\[ y = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)x - 6 \]
\[ y = \frac{x}{3} - 6 \]

Option B

Therefore, Option B is correct.
