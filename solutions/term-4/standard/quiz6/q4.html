Problem

The table provides details of a data set.

Lowest extreme 55
Lower quartile 70
Interquartile range 90
Range 170
Median 85

Which of these 5-number summaries could represent this data?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Identify the Known Summary Points

A 5-number summary always consists of five specific milestone values in order:

\[ [\text{Min},\; Q_1,\; \text{Median},\; Q_3,\; \text{Max}] \]

The table directly gives us three of these points:

  • \(\text{Lowest Extreme (Min)} = 55\)
  • \(\text{Lower Quartile }(Q_1) = 70\)
  • \(\text{Median} = 85\)

Step 2: Calculate the Upper Quartile (\( Q_3 \))

The Interquartile Range (IQR) is the distance between \( Q_1 \) and \( Q_3 \):

\[ \text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1 \]

Rearranging to solve for \( Q_3 \):

\[ Q_3 = Q_1 + \text{IQR} \]
\[ Q_3 = 70 + 90 = \mathbf{160} \]

Step 3: Calculate the Maximum & View Box Plot

The Range is the total spread from the lowest extreme to the highest extreme:

\[ \text{Range} = \text{Max} - \text{Min} \]

Rearranging to find \(\text{Max}\):

\[ \text{Max} = \text{Min} + \text{Range} \]
\[ \text{Max} = 55 + 170 = \mathbf{225} \]

55, 70, 85, 160, 225

Therefore, Option D is correct.
