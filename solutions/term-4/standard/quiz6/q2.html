Problem

An artwork is sold at a gallery for $1650, including 10% GST.

What is the price of the artwork, before GST is added?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Understand the Total Percentage

The original price before tax is our base, which represents 100%. Adding 10% GST means the final price of $1650 represents 110% of the original price.

Step 2: Method 1 - Unitary Method

If 110% is equal to $1650, we can find 1% by dividing by 110.

\[ 1\% = \frac{1650}{110} = \$15 \]

To find the original price (100%), we multiply that 1% value by 100:

\[ 100\% = \$15 \times 100 = \$1500 \]

Step 3: Method 2 - Algebraic Equation

Alternatively, let \( x \) be the original price. Adding 10% is the same as multiplying by 1.10.

\[ x \times 1.10 = 1650 \]
\[ x = \frac{1650}{1.10} \]

$1500

Both methods show that Option B is correct.
