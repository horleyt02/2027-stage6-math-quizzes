Problem

One week Brad worked 6 hours overtime, paid at time and a half rate.

He earned an extra $360.

If Brad earned a total of $1200 including overtime, how many hours did he work at the normal rate?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Find the overtime rate

We know Brad earned $360 for 6 hours of overtime. We can find his hourly overtime rate by dividing the pay by the hours:

\( \$360 \div 6 \text{ hours} = \mathbf{\$60 \text{ per hour}} \)

Step 2: Find the normal rate

The overtime rate is "time and a half" (or \( 1.5 \times \) the normal rate). To find the normal rate, we divide the overtime rate by 1.5:

\( \$60 \div 1.5 = \mathbf{\$40 \text{ per hour}} \)

Step 3: Find the normal pay

Brad's total pay was $1200. This includes his overtime. We must subtract the extra overtime pay ($360) to find how much he earned just at his normal rate:

\( \$1200 - \$360 = \mathbf{\$840 \text{ normal pay}} \)

Step 4: Calculate normal hours

Finally, we divide his normal pay by his normal hourly rate to find the number of hours worked:

\( \$840 \div \$40/\text{hr} = 21 \text{ hours} \)

21

Therefore, Option B is correct.
