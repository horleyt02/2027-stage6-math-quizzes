Problem

The thickness of a sheet of clingwrap is \( 0.0267 \text{ mm} \).

Express this in standard notation, correct to 2 significant figures.

Worked Solution

Step 1: Standard Notation Format

Standard notation (or scientific notation) is written in the form \( A \times 10^n \), where \( 1 \le A < 10 \).

To convert \( 0.0267 \) into this format, the main number needs to be \( 2.67 \).

Step 2: Find the Power of 10

While we often use the shortcut of "moving the decimal point" 2 places to the right, conceptually, what is really happening?

  • We are multiplying \( 0.0267 \) by \( 100 \) (which is \( 10^2 \)) to shift the digits and get \( 2.67 \).
  • To keep the overall value equal to the original number, we must balance this by simultaneously dividing by \( 100 \) (which is multiplying by \( 10^{-2} \)).
\( 0.0267 = \mathbf{2.67 \times 10^{-2}} \)

Step 3: Round to 2 Significant Figures

We need our answer to 2 significant figures. Looking at \( 2.67 \):

  • The first two significant digits are 2 and 6.
  • The next digit is 7.
  • Because 7 is 5 or greater, we round up the 6 to a 7.
\( 2.67 \text{ rounds to } \mathbf{2.7} \)

\( 2.7 \times 10^{-2} \text{ mm} \)

Therefore, Option A is correct.
