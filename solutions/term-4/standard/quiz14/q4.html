Problem

Melbourne is located at the coordinates \( 38^\circ\text{S} \; 145^\circ\text{E} \).

What are the coordinates of a city which is \( 55^\circ \) north and \( 40^\circ \) west of Melbourne?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Find the New Latitude (Move \( 55^\circ \) North from \( 38^\circ\text{S} \))

Starting in the Southern Hemisphere at \( 38^\circ\text{S} \), moving North takes you up toward the Equator (\( 0^\circ \)) and across into the Northern Hemisphere:

\( 38^\circ \) North brings you from \( 38^\circ\text{S} \) to the Equator (\( 0^\circ \)).
Remaining distance North: \( 55^\circ - 38^\circ = 17^\circ \) into the Northern Hemisphere.
\[ \text{New latitude} = 17^\circ\text{N} \]

Step 2: Find the New Longitude (Move \( 40^\circ \) West from \( 145^\circ\text{E} \))

Starting in the Eastern Hemisphere at \( 145^\circ\text{E} \), moving West decreases the East longitude as you move back toward the Prime Meridian (\( 0^\circ \)):

\[ 145^\circ\text{E} - 40^\circ = 105^\circ\text{E} \]
(Since we do not cross \( 0^\circ \), the city remains in the Eastern Hemisphere.)
\[ \text{New longitude} = 105^\circ\text{E} \]

Step 3: Visualise the Path on a Latitude–Longitude Grid

Tracing both steps on a coordinate grid clearly shows Melbourne crossing the Equator (\( 0^\circ \)) from South to North, then shifting West toward the Prime Meridian:

Melbourne (\( 38^\circ\text{S}, 145^\circ\text{E} \)) Target City (\( 17^\circ\text{N}, 105^\circ\text{E} \))
\[ 17^\circ\text{N}, \; 105^\circ\text{E} \]

Therefore, Option D is the correct choice.
