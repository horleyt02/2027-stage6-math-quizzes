Problem

A store owner buys a watch for \( \$150.00 \) and adds \( 30\% \) to the price for his profit margin.

What is the selling price of the watch after \( 10\% \) GST is added to the store owner's price?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Add the \( 30\% \) Profit Margin to Find the Store Owner's Price

Increasing the \( \$150.00 \) cost price by \( 30\% \) is the same as multiplying by \( 1 + 0.30 = 1.30 \):

\( \text{Profit margin} = 30\% \text{ of } \$150.00 = 0.30 \times 150 = \$45.00 \)
\[ \text{Store owner's price} = \$150.00 \times 1.30 = \$195.00 \]

Step 2: Add \( 10\% \) GST to the Store Owner's Price (\( \$195.00 \))

The \( 10\% \) GST is calculated on the new price of \( \$195.00 \) (not the original \( \$150.00 \)). Multiply by \( 1 + 0.10 = 1.10 \):

\( \text{GST} = 10\% \text{ of } \$195.00 = 0.10 \times 195 = \$19.50 \)
\[ \text{Selling price} = \$195.00 \times 1.10 = \$214.50 \]

Step 3: Visualise Successive Multipliers (\( \times 1.43 \), Not \( \times 1.40 \))

Applying two successive percentage increases means multiplying their scale factors together — which gives a \( 43\% \) total increase rather than \( 40\% \):

\[ \$150.00 \times 1.30 \times 1.10 = \$150.00 \times 1.43 = \$214.50 \]

Therefore, Option D is the correct choice.
