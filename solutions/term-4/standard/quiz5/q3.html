Problem

City \( A \) has a Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) of +8 and is currently 5:00 pm.

City \( B \) is currently 6:00 am on the same day as City \( A \).

What is the UTC of City \( B \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Convert to 24-hour time

To make finding the time difference easier, convert City A's time to a 24-hour format.

\[ 5:00\text{ pm} + 12\text{ hours} = \mathbf{17:00} \]

City B is at 6:00 am, which is simply 06:00 in 24-hour time.

Step 2: Calculate the Time Difference

Both cities are on the same day. We subtract City B's time from City A's time to find how many hours apart they are.

\[ 17:00 - 06:00 = 11\text{ hours} \]

Since 06:00 is earlier in the day than 17:00, City B is 11 hours behind City A.

Step 3: Determine City B's UTC

City A is at UTC +8. Since City B is 11 hours behind, we subtract 11 from City A's UTC offset.

\[ \text{UTC}_B = +8 - 11 \]
\[ \text{UTC}_B = -3 \]

−3

Therefore, Option C is correct.
