Problem

The surface area of a cube is 73.5 m².

What is the side length of the cube?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Understanding Surface Area

A cube is composed of exactly 6 identical square faces. The total surface area is the combined area of all 6 faces.

Step 2: Area of a Single Face

To find the area of just one face, we divide the total surface area by 6.

\[ \text{Face Area} = \frac{73.5}{6} = \mathbf{12.25 \text{ m}^2} \]

Step 3: Finding the Side Length

Each face is a square. The formula for the area of a square is:

\[ \text{Area} = \text{side}^2 \]

To find the side length, we take the square root of the face area.

\[ \text{Side Length} = \sqrt{12.25} \]

3.5 m
