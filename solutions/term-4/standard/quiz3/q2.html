Problem

The coordinates of the city of Los Angeles are approximately (34°N, 120°W).

The coordinates of Cairo are approximately (30°N, 30°E).

What is the local time in Cairo if it is 9:00 am in Los Angeles?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Extract Relevant Information

Time zones are determined exclusively by longitude (East/West). We can completely ignore the latitude (North/South) coordinates.

  • Los Angeles is at \( 120^\circ\text{W} \)
  • Cairo is at \( 30^\circ\text{E} \)

Step 2: Calculate Longitudinal Difference

Because one city is West of the Prime Meridian (\(0^\circ\)) and the other is East, we must add their values together to find the total longitudinal distance between them.

Step 3: Convert Degrees to Hours

Look at your reference sheet. Under "Time and location", you are given the conversion fact:

\( 15^\circ = 1 \text{ hour time difference} \)

Divide our total longitudinal difference by 15 to find the hours:

\[ 150 \div 15 = \mathbf{10 \text{ hours difference}} \]

Step 4: Calculate Local Time

Cairo is East of Los Angeles. Because the Earth rotates Eastward, locations to the East are ahead in time.

Therefore, we must add 10 hours to the time in Los Angeles.

9:00 am + 10 hours = 7:00 pm

7:00 pm

Therefore, Option C is correct.
