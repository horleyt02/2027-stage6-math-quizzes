Problem

A sample of 25 residents were asked to indicate the time, in hours, spent using the internet across the past weekend. The results are displayed in the following graph.

What is the median number in hours?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Understand the Median

The median is the middle value when all the data points are placed in numerical order from smallest to largest.

A great way to find the median on a dot plot is to use the "crossing off" method: cross off the lowest dot, then the highest dot, and keep pairing them up working towards the centre.

Step 2: Find the Middle Position

We are told there are 25 residents in total (\( n = 25 \)).

Because 25 is an odd number, there will be exactly one middle dot. If we cross off 12 dots from the lowest end, and 12 dots from the highest end (using 24 dots), the 13th dot remaining will be the median.

Step 3: Visual "Crossing Off"

Let's cross off the lowest 12 dots (grey) and the highest 12 dots (grey). The remaining dot (green) is our median.

Step 4: Identify the Value

The 13th dot sits in the column above 5 on the x-axis.

5.0

Therefore, Option C is correct.
