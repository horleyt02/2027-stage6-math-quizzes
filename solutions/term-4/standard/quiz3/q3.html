Problem

Sarah is paid an annual salary of $96,200 and has 22% of her salary deducted for income tax. She also has fixed weekly deductions of $28.50 for professional association fees and $64.50 for private health insurance.

What is her weekly net pay?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Calculate Weekly Gross Pay

Because the final answer asks for the weekly net pay, it is easiest to convert her annual salary into a weekly gross amount first. There are 52 weeks in a year.

\( \$96,200 \div 52 = \mathbf{\$1,850} \text{ per week} \)

Step 2: Calculate Weekly Tax

Income tax is calculated as a percentage of the gross pay. We need to find 22% of her weekly earnings:

\( 0.22 \times \$1,850 = \mathbf{\$407} \text{ tax per week} \)

Step 3: Total Deductions

Add up all the weekly deductions that will be taken out of her pay:

  • Income Tax: \( \$407.00 \)
  • Association Fees: \( \$28.50 \)
  • Health Insurance: \( \$64.50 \)
Total Deductions = \( \mathbf{\$500.00} \)

Step 4: Calculate Net Pay

Net pay is the "take-home" pay, calculated by subtracting all deductions from the gross pay:

\( \$1,850 - \$500 = \mathbf{\$1,350} \)

$1350

Therefore, Option A is correct.
