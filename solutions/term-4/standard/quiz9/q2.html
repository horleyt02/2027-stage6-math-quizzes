Problem

The composite figure below consists of a rhombus with a semicircle on two of the opposite sides.

The rhombus has a side length of 6 cm and a perpendicular height of 3 cm.

Not To Scale

What is the total area of the composite figure? (Round your answer to one decimal place.)

Worked Solution

Step 1: Area of the Rhombus

The area of a rhombus can be found using the formula \( \text{Area} = \text{base} \times \text{height} \).

\[ \text{Area}_{\text{rhombus}} = 6 \text{ cm} \times 3 \text{ cm} \]
\[ \text{Area}_{\text{rhombus}} = 18 \text{ cm}^2 \]

Step 2: Area of the Semicircles

There are two identical semicircles attached to the sides of the rhombus, which together form exactly one full circle.

  • The diameter is the side of the rhombus: \( d = 6 \text{ cm} \).
  • The radius is half the diameter: \( r = 3 \text{ cm} \).
\[ \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} = \pi \times r^2 \]
\[ \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} = \pi \times 3^2 = 9\pi \]
\[ \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} \approx 28.274 \text{ cm}^2 \]

Step 3: Calculate Total Area

Add the area of the central rhombus and the circular components together, then round to one decimal place.

\[ \text{Total Area} = 18 + 28.274 \]
\[ \text{Total Area} = 46.274 \]
