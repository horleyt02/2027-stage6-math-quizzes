Problem

The two mathematics classes in the Year 11 mathematics standard course sat for the same assessment task and the results are shown in the table.

Class Number of
students		 Mean (%)
11MS1 25 62
11MS2 20 80

What is the mean mark for the course?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Find Total Marks for Each Class

To find the combined mean, we first need to know the total sum of all marks. Multiply the number of students in each class by their respective mean.

11MS1 Total: \( 25 \times 62 = 1550 \)
11MS2 Total: \( 20 \times 80 = 1600 \)

Step 2: Find Combined Totals

Add the total marks together, and add the total number of students together.

Total Marks: \( 1550 + 1600 = 3150 \)
Total Students: \( 25 + 20 = 45 \)

Step 3: Calculate Combined Mean

Divide the combined total marks by the total number of students in the course.

\[ \text{Mean} = \frac{3150}{45} = 70 \]

70

Therefore, Option B is correct.
