Problem

In the expansion of \( (2x + 7)^8 \), the first term is \( 256x^8 \).

What is the sixth term?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Establish the Expansion Order

Look at the binomial expansion formula on your reference sheet: \( (x + y)^n = ^nC_0 x^n + \dots \)

We are given that the first term is \( 256x^8 \). Let's verify this matches the standard order:

\[ ^8C_0 (2x)^8 (7)^0 = 1 \cdot (256x^8) \cdot 1 = 256x^8 \]

Because it matches perfectly, we know the expansion follows descending powers of \( (2x) \) and ascending powers of \( 7 \).

Step 2: Find 'r' for the Sixth Term

The general term for a binomial expansion is given by \( T_{r+1} = \ ^nC_r (a)^{n-r} (b)^r \).

To find the 6th term, we set \( r + 1 = 6 \).
Therefore, \( r = 5 \).

Step 3: Substitute and Evaluate

Substitute our known values (\( n=8, r=5, a=2x, b=7 \)) into the general term formula.

\[ T_6 = \ ^8C_5 (2x)^{8-5} (7)^5 \]
\[ T_6 = \ ^8C_5 (2x)^3 (7)^5 \]

Therefore, Option C is correct.
