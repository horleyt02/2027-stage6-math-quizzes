Problem

For what value of \( k \) does the polynomial \( P(x) = x^2 + 13x + 36 \) leave a remainder of \( k \) when divided by \( (x - k) \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: State the Remainder Theorem

The Remainder Theorem states that when a polynomial \( P(x) \) is divided by \( (x - k) \), the remainder is given by evaluating \( P(k) \).

The problem tells us the remainder is \( k \). Therefore, \( P(k) = k \).

Step 2: Set up the equation

Substitute \( x = k \) into the polynomial \( P(x) = x^2 + 13x + 36 \) and set it equal to \( k \):

\[ k^2 + 13k + 36 = k \]

Step 3: Rearrange the quadratic

To solve the quadratic equation, subtract \( k \) from both sides so the equation equals zero:

\[ k^2 + 13k - k + 36 = 0 \]
\[ k^2 + 12k + 36 = 0 \]

Step 4: Factor and solve

Recognise that this is a perfect square trinomial:

\[ (k + 6)(k + 6) = 0 \]
\[ (k + 6)^2 = 0 \]

\( k = -6 \)

Therefore, Option A is correct.
