Problem

What is the term in \( x^5 \) in the expression of \( (1 + x)^8(1 - x) \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Strategize the Multiplication

We need to find the specific parts of the expansion of \( (1+x)^8 \) that will create an \( x^5 \) term when multiplied by \( (1-x) \).

To get an \( x^5 \) term from \( [\dots \text{expansion} \dots] \times (1 - x) \), we need:
  • The \( x^5 \) term from the expansion multiplied by \( 1 \)
  • The \( x^4 \) term from the expansion multiplied by \( -x \)

Step 2: Find the Binomial Coefficients

Using the binomial expansion formula from the reference sheet:

\[ (x+y)^n = {}^nC_0 x^n + \dots + {}^nC_r x^{n-r}y^r + \dots + {}^nC_n y^n \]

For \( (1+x)^8 \), the general term is \( {}^8C_r (1)^{8-r} (x)^r = {}^8C_r x^r \).

Find the \( x^5 \) term (\( r = 5 \)):
\[ {}^8C_5 x^5 = 56x^5 \]
Find the \( x^4 \) term (\( r = 4 \)):
\[ {}^8C_4 x^4 = 70x^4 \]

Step 3: Combine to Find the Final Term

Substitute these terms back into our multiplication strategy from Step 1:

\[ (56x^5 + 70x^4 + \dots)(1 - x) \]
Multiply to isolate the \(x^5\) components:
\[ = (56x^5)(1) + (70x^4)(-x) \]
\[ = 56x^5 - 70x^5 \]
\[ = -14x^5 \]

\[ -14x^5 \]

Therefore, Option B is correct.
