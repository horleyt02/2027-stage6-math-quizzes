Problem

How many distinct arrangements of the letters of the word RESTLESS are possible?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Count the total letters

Count the total number of letters in the word "RESTLESS".

There are 8 letters in total, so \( n = 8 \).

Step 2: Identify repeated letters

Find any letters that appear more than once, as identical items reduce the number of distinct arrangements.

R E S T L E S S
  • The letter 'E' repeats 2 times.
  • The letter 'S' repeats 3 times.

Step 3: State the formula

The number of distinct arrangements of \( n \) items where some items are identical is given by dividing the total factorial by the factorials of the repeated frequencies:

\[ \frac{n!}{n_1! n_2! \dots} \]

Step 4: Calculate

Substitute our values into the formula and evaluate:

\[ \frac{8!}{2! \times 3!} \]
\[ = \frac{40320}{2 \times 6} = \frac{40320}{12} \]

\( = 3360 \)

Therefore, Option A is correct.
