Problem

The graphs of \( y = f(x) \) and \( y = g(x) \) are shown below.

Below is the graph of \( y = h(x) \).

Which is a correct equation for \( h(x) \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Analyze the Roots

Look at the \( x \)-intercepts (roots) of the functions.

  • \( f(x) \) has roots at \( x = -3, 2, 3 \).
  • \( g(x) \) has a root at \( x = 0 \).
The graph of \( h(x) \) has roots at all of these locations: \( \mathbf{x = -3, 0, 2, 3} \). This strongly suggests \( h(x) \) is composed of the combined factors of both functions.

Step 2: Analyze the Degrees

Evaluate the shapes of the polynomials to determine their degrees.

  • \( f(x) \) has 3 roots and 2 turning points, so it is a cubic (degree 3).
  • \( g(x) \) is a parabola (degree 2).
  • \( h(x) \) has 4 turning points and opposite end behavior, making it a quintic (degree 5).
Addition or subtraction would maintain a maximum degree of 3. Only multiplication adds degrees (\( 3 + 2 = 5 \)).

Step 3: Verify the y-intercept

Check the \( y \)-intercept where \( x = 0 \).

From the graphs, \( f(0) = 9 \) and \( g(0) = 0 \). The graph of \( h(x) \) passes exactly through the origin, so \( h(0) = 0 \).

Addition:
\( f(0) + g(0) = 9 + 0 = 9 \neq 0 \)
Subtraction:
\( f(0) - g(0) = 9 - 0 = 9 \neq 0 \)
Multiplication:
\( f(0) \times g(0) = 9 \times 0 = 0 \)

Step 4: Conclusion

Since the roots, degrees, and \( y \)-intercept all align perfectly with multiplication, the correct operation is multiplication.

\( h(x) = f(x)g(x) \)

Therefore, Option A is correct.
