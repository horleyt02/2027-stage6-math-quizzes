Problem

Which Cartesian equation is equivalent to the parametric equations

\[ x = 3t + 2 \quad \text{and} \quad y = t^2 \text{ ?} \]

Worked Solution

Step 1: Isolate the Parameter \( t \)

To find the Cartesian equation (which only involves \( x \) and \( y \)), we must eliminate the parameter \( t \). Start by rearranging the linear equation to solve for \( t \):

\[ x = 3t + 2 \]
\[ x - 2 = 3t \]
\[ t = \frac{x - 2}{3} \]

Step 2: Substitute into the Second Equation

Now, substitute this expression for \( t \) directly into the equation for \( y \):

\[ y = \left( \frac{x - 2}{3} \right)^2 \]

Step 3: Expand and Simplify

To expand this, we must square both the numerator and the denominator.

Perfect Square Expansion Reminder:
\[ (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \]
\[ y = \frac{(x - 2)^2}{3^2} \]
\[ y = \frac{x^2 - 2(x)(2) + 2^2}{9} \]
\[ y = \frac{x^2 - 4x + 4}{9} \]

Option D

Therefore, Option D is correct.
