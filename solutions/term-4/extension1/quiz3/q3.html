Problem

Out of 10 job applicants, seven are selected to move on to the final interview round. From those seven, three will be chosen and assigned to the positions of Manager, Supervisor and Coordinator.

In how many ways can this process be carried out?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Selecting the Finalists

First, we must select 7 people from the 10 applicants to move on to the final round. Because the order they are selected in does not matter at this stage, we use a combination.

Refer to the reference sheet for \( ^nC_r \):

\[ ^{10}C_7 = \frac{10!}{7!(10 - 7)!} = \mathbf{\frac{10!}{7!3!}} \]

Step 2: Assigning the Jobs

Next, from these 7 finalists, 3 are chosen for the positions of Manager, Supervisor, and Coordinator. Because these are distinct roles, assigning person A as Manager and person B as Supervisor is different from B as Manager and A as Supervisor. Order matters, so we use a permutation.

Refer to the reference sheet for \( ^nP_r \):

\[ ^7P_3 = \frac{7!}{(7 - 3)!} = \mathbf{\frac{7!}{4!}} \]

Step 3: Multiply and Simplify

To find the total number of ways the entire process can be carried out, we multiply the number of ways from Step 1 by the number of ways from Step 2:

\[ \text{Total Ways} = \frac{10!}{7!3!} \times \frac{7!}{4!} \]
Notice that the \( 7! \) in the denominator divides the \( 7! \) in the numerator:
\[ = \frac{10!}{\cancel{7!}3!} \times \frac{\cancel{7!}}{4!} \]
\[ = \frac{10!}{3!4!} \]

Option B

Therefore, Option B is correct.
