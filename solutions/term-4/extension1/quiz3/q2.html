Problem

Consider the graph.

Which of the following best represents the equation of the graph?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Identify the Roots (Factors)

Look at where the graph crosses or touches the \( x \)-axis.

  • Root at \( x = -3 \implies \) factor is \( (x + 3) \)
  • Root at \( x = 0 \implies \) factor is \( x \)
  • Root at \( x = 2 \implies \) factor is \( (x - 2) \)

Step 2: Determine Multiplicities

Look at the shape of the graph at each root to find the power of each factor.

  • At \( x = -3 \), the graph bounces off the axis (turning point). This means it has an even multiplicity, like \( \boldsymbol{(x + 3)^2}\).
  • At \( x = 0 \), the graph flattens out but crosses the axis (point of inflection). This means it has an odd multiplicity greater than 1, like \( \boldsymbol{x^3} \).
  • At \( x = 2 \), the graph bounces off the axis. This means it has an even multiplicity, like \( \boldsymbol{(x - 2)^2} \).

Step 3: Analyse End Behaviour

Look at the tails of the graph.

  • The degree of our polynomial is \( 2 + 3 + 2 = 7 \) (an odd degree).
  • The graph starts at the bottom-left and ends at the top-right.
An odd-degree polynomial that ends in the positive \( y \)-direction (top-right) must have a positive leading coefficient.

Step 4: Conclusion

Combining the factors, multiplicities, and a positive leading coefficient, we get an equation in the form:

\[ y = a x^3 (x + 3)^2 (x - 2)^2 \]

Where \( a \) is a positive number. Looking at the options, only Option A fits all these criteria.
