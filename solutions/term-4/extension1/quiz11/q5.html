Problem

How many unique ways can the letters of the word JUSTIFICATIONS be arranged?

J U S T I F I C A T I O N S

Worked Solution

Step 1: Count Total Letters

First, determine the total number of items to be arranged (\( n \)).

The word JUSTIFICATIONS has exactly 14 letters.
Numerator = \( 14! \)

Step 2: Identify Repeated Letters

Because swapping identical letters (like the two 'S's) doesn't create a new unique word, we must identify and count all repetitions.

  • The letter S appears 2 times.
  • The letter T appears 2 times.
  • The letter I appears 3 times.

Step 3: Apply the Formula

The formula for permutations with indistinguishable items is to divide the total permutations (\( n! \)) by the factorial of the count of each repeated item.

\[ \frac{n!}{n_1! \times n_2! \times \dots} \]
\[ \frac{14!}{2! \times 2! \times 3!} \]

Therefore, Option C is correct.
