Problem

What is the coefficient of \( x^6 \) in the expansion of \( (2x - 1)^9 \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Write the General Term from the Reference Sheet

From the reference sheet, the binomial expansion is:

\[ (x + y)^n = {^nC_0}x^n + {^nC_1}x^{n-1}y + {^nC_2}x^{n-2}y^2 + \cdots + {^nC_n}y^n \]
For \( (2x - 1)^9 \), the general term \( T_{r+1} \) is:
\[ T_{r+1} = {^9C_r}(2x)^{9-r}(-1)^r \]

Step 2: Find the Value of \( r \) for the \( x^6 \) Term

We need the power of \( x \) to be \( 6 \), so set the exponent \( 9 - r \) equal to \( 6 \):

\[ 9 - r = 6 \implies r = 3 \]
Substitute \( r = 3 \) into the general term:
\[ T_4 = {^9C_3}(2x)^6(-1)^3 \]

Step 3: Evaluate Each Factor to Find the Coefficient

Remember to raise the entire term \( (2x) \) to the power of \( 6 \), so \( (2x)^6 = 2^6 x^6 = 64x^6 \):

\[ {^9C_3} = 84, \qquad 2^6 = 64, \qquad (-1)^3 = -1 \]
\[ T_4 = 84 \times 64x^6 \times (-1) = -5376x^6 \]
\[ \text{Coefficient of } x^6 = -5376 \]

Therefore, Option A is the correct choice.
