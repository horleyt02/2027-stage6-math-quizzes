Problem

Which of the following is an inverse function to \( f(x) = x^2 - 8x + 16 \)?

Worked Solution

Step 1: Factorise \( f(x) \) as a Perfect Square

Notice that \( x^2 - 8x + 16 \) is a perfect square trinomial. Rewrite \( y = f(x) \) in completed-square form so \( x \) appears only once:

\[ y = x^2 - 8x + 16 \]
\[ y = (x - 4)^2 \]

Step 2: Swap \( x \) and \( y \), then Isolate \( y \)

Swap \( x \) and \( y \) to find the inverse relation, then reverse the operations in the correct order (take the square root first, then add \( 4 \)):

\[ x = (y - 4)^2 \]
\[ \pm\sqrt{x} = y - 4 \]
\[ y = 4 \pm \sqrt{x} \]

Restricting the domain of \( f(x) \) to \( x \ge 4 \) selects the positive branch, giving the inverse function \( f^{-1}(x) = \sqrt{x} + 4 \).

Step 3: Confirm Graphically by Reflecting Across \( y = x \)

Reflecting the right branch of \( y = (x - 4)^2 \) (for \( x \ge 4 \)) across the line \( y = x \) maps the vertex \( (4, 0) \) to the endpoint \( (0, 4) \) of \( y = \sqrt{x} + 4 \):

\( f(x) = (x - 4)^2 \; (x \ge 4) \) \( f^{-1}(x) = \sqrt{x} + 4 \) \( y = x \)

Therefore, Option A is the correct choice.
