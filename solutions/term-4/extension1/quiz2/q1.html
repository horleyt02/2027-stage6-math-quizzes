Problem

Which of the following is the set of all solutions to the inequality?

\[ |x - 3| \ge 7 \]

Worked Solution

Step 1: Set up the algebraic cases

An absolute value inequality of the form \( |X| \ge k \) splits into two separate inequalities connected by "OR". We must account for both the positive and negative scenarios.

Rule: \( X \ge k \quad \text{OR} \quad X \le -k \)

Step 2: Write the two inequalities

Apply the rule from Step 1 to our specific problem. Remember to reverse the inequality sign when creating the negative case!

\( x - 3 \ge 7 \) OR \( x - 3 \le -7 \)

Step 3: Solve each inequality

Add 3 to both sides of each inequality to solve for \( x \):

\( x \ge 10 \) OR \( x \le -4 \)

Step 4: Convert to interval notation & Graph

Translate these inequalities into interval notation. Since the inequalities are "or equal to" (\( \ge \), \( \le \)), we use square brackets for the numbers.

\( (-\infty, -4] \cup [10, \infty) \)

Therefore, Option B is correct.
