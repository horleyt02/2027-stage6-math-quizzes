Problem

What is the expansion of the following?

\[ (2\sqrt{3} - 3)^3 \]

Worked Solution

Step 1: The Binomial Formula & Pascal's Triangle

The reference sheet provides the binomial expansion formula using combinations (\(^nC_r\)). For a power of 3, the coefficients \(^3C_0, ^3C_1, ^3C_2, ^3C_3\) correspond exactly to the 3rd row of Pascal's Triangle.

\[ (x + y)^3 = \mathbf{1}x^3 + \mathbf{3}x^2y + \mathbf{3}xy^2 + \mathbf{1}y^3 \]

Step 2: Substitute Values

Identify your terms: \( x = 2\sqrt{3} \) and \( y = -3 \). Substitute these into the formula carefully using brackets.

\[ 1(2\sqrt{3})^3 + 3(2\sqrt{3})^2(-3) + 3(2\sqrt{3})(-3)^2 + 1(-3)^3 \]

Step 3: Evaluate Each Term

Calculate each of the four terms separately:

  • Term 1: \( (2\sqrt{3})^3 = 2^3 \times (\sqrt{3})^3 = 8 \times 3\sqrt{3} = \mathbf{24\sqrt{3}} \)
  • Term 2: \( 3(2\sqrt{3})^2(-3) = 3(4 \times 3)(-3) = 3(12)(-3) = \mathbf{-108} \)
  • Term 3: \( 3(2\sqrt{3})(-3)^2 = 3(2\sqrt{3})(9) = \mathbf{54\sqrt{3}} \)
  • Term 4: \( (-3)^3 = \mathbf{-27} \)

Step 4: Combine Like Terms

Add the evaluated terms together, grouping the surds and the integers:

\[ 24\sqrt{3} - 108 + 54\sqrt{3} - 27 \]
\[ (24\sqrt{3} + 54\sqrt{3}) + (-108 - 27) \]

\( 78\sqrt{3} - 135 \)

Therefore, Option D is correct.
